Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026 · applies to Captura v7.0.0 and later

This is the policy shown inside the app under Settings → About → Privacy policy, with links and the retention and third-party sections added.

What Captura does with your data

Captura is published by Like Services Group Pty Ltd. It is a screen recorder and media editor. Everything you capture or edit — screen recordings, screenshots, photos, audio — is stored on your device only. Captura has no account system and never uploads your captures anywhere.

On-device processing

Privacy Blur detects text (one-time codes, phone numbers, email addresses, card numbers) and faces using Google ML Kit models that run on your device. Photo background removal uses an on-device matting model. No image or video frame leaves your phone for these features.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Screen capture: to record the screen (requested each time you start recording).
  • Microphone: to record your voice alongside the screen or as a standalone recording.
  • Camera: for the facecam bubble and camera recordings.
  • Display over other apps: for the floating record button and facecam bubble.
  • Notifications: for the recording controls while Captura runs in the background.
  • Photos / media: to open your existing files in the editors and to save results to your gallery when you enable it in Settings → Storage.

Diagnostic logs

Captura keeps a short anonymous diagnostic log on your device to help troubleshoot problems. It contains no captures and is not uploaded. You can turn it off in Settings → Privacy → Diagnostic logging.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and app-usage signals to show and measure ads, under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Captura Pro removes all ads.

Purchases

Captura Pro is sold through Google Play Billing as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Captura never sees your payment details; Google processes the purchase and tells the app only whether a subscription or lifetime purchase is active.

Dynamic content

Filters, stickers, fonts and other creative content may be refreshed from a content server. These requests download signed content catalogues only; they do not send your captures, identity or usage data.

Data retention and deletion

Like Services Group Pty Ltd does not store any of your data on a server. Captures saved by Captura live in your device's local storage, under your control, and are deleted when you delete them or uninstall the app. Advertising ID and related ad data held by Google are subject to Google's own retention policies and your device's ad-ID controls. If you believe we hold any data about you in connection with Captura, contact the email below and the request will be actioned within 30 days.

Third-party services

Captura relies on the following Google services. Their own privacy policies govern any data they collect:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services (including Google ML Kit on-device models)
  • Google Play Billing

Children

Captura is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
